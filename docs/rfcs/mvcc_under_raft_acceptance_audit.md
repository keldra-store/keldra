## Audit conclusion

Criteria 1–17 and all 16 Section 27 invariants have identifiable production
implementations and executable test coverage. Their validation remains pending
until the current tree is compiled and the cited tests are run. Criterion 18
does not require a performance scenario for every correctness invariant; it
requires the combined suite to demonstrate them. The remaining criterion-18
gap is narrower: several required Section 29 benchmark names exist, but their
current harness paths use in-memory mocks or substitute a different operation,
so they do not yet measure the behavior their names promise.
